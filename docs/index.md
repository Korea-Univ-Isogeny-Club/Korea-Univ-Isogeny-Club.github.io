## Featured topic

### What is an isogeny?

An **isogeny** is a non-constant morphism of elliptic curves that preserves the identity. It is simultaneously an algebraic map, a group homomorphism, and a bridge between the arithmetic of two curves.

\[
  \phi : E_1 \longrightarrow E_2, \qquad \phi(\mathcal O_{E_1}) = \mathcal O_{E_2}.
\]
