# Endomorphism

!!! definition "Definition (Endomorphism)"

    Let $K$ is an extension of $K'$.
    Let $E_1/K'$ and $E_2/K'$ are [elliptic curves](../definitions/elliptic_curve.md) over a field $K'$.

    Let $\phi: E_1/K' \to E_2/K'$ be an [isogeny](../definitions/isogeny.md) over $K$. If $E_1$ and $E_2$ are isomorphic over $K$, the isogeny $\phi$ is called **endomorphism**.
